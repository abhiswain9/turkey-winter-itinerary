Istanbul
Dec 25 – Jan 3

A 9-night winter escape focused on historic Sultanahmet, the Bosphorus, New Year’s Eve fireworks, food, and cozy neighborhood wandering.

📍 Base: Istanbul 🌡️ 5–11°C • Rain/Snow possible 🎆 NYE on Bosphorus

Trip Snapshot

🕌

Focus

Istanbul’s historic peninsula + modern Beyoğlu, with optional Asian-side and neighborhood deep dives. Pure Istanbul keeps the pace relaxed and weather-flexible.

🎉

Highlight

New Year’s Eve fireworks over the Bosphorus. Book a dinner cruise, rooftop, or waterfront spot weeks ahead — this is the big event.

❄️

Season

Cold, short days, possible rain or light snow. Fewer crowds than summer, lower hotel rates (except NYE week). Perfect for museums, hammams & food.

  • • Arrive Dec 25 → Depart Jan 3 (9 nights)
  • • Stay in Sultanahmet or Beyoğlu/Galata for walkability
  • • Get an Istanbulkart for trams, metro, ferries & buses
  • • Optional: 1–2 night Cappadocia add-on (flights ~1.5 h) if you want fairy chimneys
  • • Book NYE dinner / cruise as early as possible

Practical Tips

🌡️ Weather & Packing Mindset

Expect daytime 5–11 °C, nights colder. Rain is common; snow is possible but not guaranteed. Wind off the Bosphorus makes it feel colder. Waterproof coat + layers + grippy shoes are non-negotiable for cobblestones.

🚇 Getting Around

Buy an Istanbulkart (available at airports, stations, kiosks). Covers metro, tram, bus, ferry, funicular. Taxis and Uber/BiTaksi are plentiful. Walking is excellent in historic areas.

💰 Money & Costs

Currency: Turkish Lira (TRY). Cards widely accepted; carry some cash for small vendors and tips. Mid-range daily budget (food + transport + sights) roughly €60–120 pp depending on dining style. NYE will be higher.

🎆 New Year’s Eve

Fireworks light up the Bosphorus from multiple points. Popular viewing: Ortaköy, Beşiktaş, Galata, or a dinner cruise. Street celebrations in Taksim get very crowded. Book restaurants and cruises weeks ahead.

Optional Add-ons

Cappadocia

1.5 h flight. Better as 1–2 nights for balloons & valleys (weather dependent in winter). Day trips exist but are long.

Princes’ Islands

Ferry day trip for quiet walking, bikes, and sea air. Check winter schedules.

Hammam

Classic winter treat. Historic options like Cağaloğlu or newer spa-style baths. Book popular ones in advance.
