Sample Itinerary

Istanbul
Dec 25 – Jan 3

A 9-night winter escape focused on historic Sultanahmet, the Bosphorus, New Year’s Eve fireworks, food, and cozy neighborhood wandering.

📍 Base: Istanbul 🌡️ 5–11°C • Rain/Snow possible 🎆 NYE on Bosphorus

Trip Snapshot

🕌

Focus

Istanbul’s historic peninsula + modern Beyoğlu, with optional Asian-side and neighborhood deep dives.

🎉

Highlight

New Year’s Eve fireworks over the Bosphorus. Book a dinner cruise, rooftop, or waterfront spot weeks ahead.

❄️

Season

Cold, short days, possible rain or light snow. Perfect for museums, hammams & food.

Quick Notes

  • • Arrive Dec 25 → Depart Jan 3 (9 nights)
  • • Two flight journeys: outbound (home → IST) and return (IST → home)
  • • Stay in Sultanahmet or Beyoğlu/Galata for walkability
  • • Get an Istanbulkart for trams, metro, ferries & buses
  • • Book NYE dinner / cruise as early as possible
  • • Most major sights open year-round

Practical Tips

Weather

Expect 5–11 °C, rain common, snow possible. Waterproof coat + layers + grippy shoes.

Getting Around

Buy an Istanbulkart. Covers metro, tram, bus, ferry, funicular.

Money

Turkish Lira (TRY). Cards widely accepted; carry some cash.

New Year’s Eve

Fireworks on the Bosphorus. Book restaurants and cruises weeks ahead.
